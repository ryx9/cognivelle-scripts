# Cold-Calling Script: Spas, Med Spas & Wellness Centers

> **Track A**: Professional Website & Digital Booking System  
> Built strictly adhering to [`skill.md`](../skill.md) specifications.

---

## Prospect Context

* **Business:** [Spa / Med Spa / Wellness Center Name]
* **Industry:** Spa, Esthetics & Wellness
* **Location:** [City, State]
* **Website:** [Outdated website / Social media page only / Website with no direct booking]
* **Booking System:** [Direct Messages (Instagram/Facebook), phone calls, or manual text requests]
* **Relevant Observation:** Clients discover the spa online or on social platforms but have to message back-and-forth or call during business hours to see open treatment slots and book.

---

## Objective

Secure permission for a 10-minute walkthrough/demo displaying an elegant spa website with a seamless treatment selection and self-serve booking system.

---

## Gatekeeper Strategy (Spa Coordinator / Front Desk)

**Salesperson:**
> "Hi, good afternoon! This is [Rep] with [Company]. I was hoping to speak with whoever oversees your website, social bookings, or client scheduling—would that be the owner or spa manager?"

*(If asked what the call is about:)*
> "I'm calling about how clients view your treatment menu and book appointments online. I noticed a quick detail on your page and wanted to share it with the manager."

---

## Primary Script

### 1. Opening & Reason for Call
**Salesperson:**
> "Hi [Owner / Manager Name], this is [Rep] with [Company]. I know I'm catching you in the middle of your day, but the reason I'm reaching out is we design elegant websites and digital booking systems for local spas and wellness studios."

### 2. Specific Observation
**Salesperson:**
> "I came across [Spa Name] on [Google / Instagram] and loved your services, but I noticed when prospective clients find you, they currently have to [send an Instagram DM / call the front desk] to check availability and book a treatment."

### 3. Light Discovery Question
**Salesperson:**
> "Are most of your appointments still coming in through phone calls and direct messages, or do you already have an online booking system set up?"

---

## Dialogue & Qualification Flow

### Branch A: "Yes, people mostly message us on Instagram or call during the day."
**Salesperson:**
> "Got it. That usually means your team spends quite a bit of time messaging back and forth just to find a time slot that works. What we do is build an elegant website where clients can view your full treatment list, pick their esthetician or therapist, and instantly book an open slot—even late at night. It saves your team hours of manual coordination."
> 
> "Would you be open to taking a quick 10-minute look at how that could work for [Spa Name]?"

### Branch B: "We already have a website."
**Salesperson:**
> "That's great. Are you happy with how it's representing your spa, or is there anything about the layout or booking side you'd want to improve?"
> 
> *(If satisfied with current design:)*  
> "Understood. The main reason I called wasn't to change your brand look, but to see if clients can easily book treatments straight from the site without having to call in. Is online scheduling something you'd like to integrate?"

### Branch C: "We already use a booking system (e.g., Vagaro, Mindbody, Fresha, Boulevard)."
**Salesperson:**
> "Understood! Are you and your clients happy with that platform, or do you find the monthly fees and client booking experience could be smoother?"
> 
> *(If they are completely satisfied:)*  
> "That makes total sense. If your current software is working great for you, I wouldn't suggest replacing it. Thank you for your time, and enjoy the rest of your day."
> 
> *(If they mention high commission fees, complexity, or poor mobile design:)*  
> "I hear that a lot—many platforms take recurring commissions or feel clunky on mobile devices. We help spas keep their custom branding and simplify the appointment flow so clients book in under a minute. Would you be open to a 10-minute comparison walkthrough?"

---

## Objection Branches

### If they say "I'm not interested."
**Salesperson:**
> "Completely understand. Just so I don't follow up unnecessarily—is it that you're fully booked through word of mouth, or is online booking just not a focus right now?"
> 
> *(If not a priority:)*  
> "Totally fair. Thanks for your time today, [Name]."

### If they ask "How much is this going to cost?"
**Salesperson:**
> "It depends on how many service categories and staff members you want to set up, but we focus on upfront, accessible pricing without eating into your service revenue with recurring booking commissions. If you have 10 minutes, I can show you a live preview and give you an exact quote tailored to your setup."

### If they say "Just send me an email with details."
**Salesperson:**
> "Happy to do that! To make sure I send something tailored rather than generic information, are you looking more for a refreshed website design, or specifically for an automated booking system to cut down on DMs and phone tag?"
> 
> *(After response:)*  
> "Perfect. What's the best email address to send that over to you?"

### If they say "I'm with a client / I'm busy right now."
**Salesperson:**
> "Understood, I won't hold you. In short: we help spas automate their appointment scheduling with an elegant website so clients can book 24/7 without phone tag. Could I give you a brief 5-minute call on [Day] at [Time]?"

### If they ask "Where is your team based?"
**Salesperson:**
> "We are based in the United States, and our development and design team is located in Pakistan. That structure lets us offer fully customized web design and booking integrations at a fraction of typical agency costs while maintaining dedicated US client support."

---

**Salesperson:**
> "Would you be open to a 10-minute walkthrough on [Day] at [Time] to see what an online booking setup would look like for [Spa Name]?"

**Salesperson:**
